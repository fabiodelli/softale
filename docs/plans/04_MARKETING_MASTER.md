# 04. MARKETING & LAUNCH MASTER PLAN 🚀
*The definitive guide to Go-To-Market, Growth Channels, and Launch Phases.*

---

## 1. LAUNCH STRATEGY: "Ship Fast, Iterate"

### Phase 1: PRE-LAUNCH (Current - Weeks 1-3)
*   **Obiettivo**: Generare catalogo minimo (30 asset) e validare tech.
*   **Checklist**:
    *   [x] Branding & Pricing definiti.
    *   [ ] 10 Storie (Level 3) prodotte.
    *   [ ] Landing Page (`/vision`) ottimizzata conversion.
    *   [ ] Stripe Live Mode attivo.

### Phase 2: SOFT LAUNCH / FOUNDER ACCESS (Weeks 4-5)
*   **Target**: 100 "Founding Members".
*   **Offerta**: Life-time Price Lock (€4.99).
*   **Canali**:
    *   Reddit (r/sleep, r/productivity, r/SaaS) - "Building in public".
    *   Twitter/X (Dev logs).
    *   Friends & Family beta.

### Phase 3: PUBLIC LAUNCH (Weeks 6-8)
*   **Target**: Product Hunt Launch Day.
*   **Canali**:
    *   Product Hunt (Launch Kit: Video, Screenshots, Maker Story).
    *   Hacker News ("Show HN").
    *   Directory submissions (Betalist, etc.).

---

## 2. GROWTH CHANNELS (Zero Budget) 📢

1.  **Content Marketing (SEO/Blog)**:
    *   Articoli su "Sleep Hygiene", "NSDR benefits", "Focus music".
    *   Intercettare intenti di ricerca specifici.
2.  **Viral Loops**:
    *   **Instagram/TikTok Reels**: Clip audio di 15s con visual rilassante.
    *   "Send a sleep story to a friend" (Gift link).
3.  **Community Engineering**:
    *   Discord per feedback diretto.
    *   Coinvolgere gli utenti nella scelta delle prossime storie ("Vota il prossimo viaggio").
